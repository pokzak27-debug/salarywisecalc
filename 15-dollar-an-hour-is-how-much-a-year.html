What $15 an Hour Means

$15 hourly full-time works out to roughly $2,600 per month before taxes. This rate matches the federal minimum wage in some states and is common for part-time or entry-level positions. Overtime, tips, or a different schedule will change your actual yearly income.

FAQ

How much is $15 an hour per year after taxes?

After federal taxes (approximate), $15/hr full-time nets about $27,000–28,000 per year. State taxes and deductions will lower your actual take-home.

What does $15 an hour equal weekly?

At 40 hours per week, $15/hr gives you $600 gross per week before any deductions.

Is $15 an hour enough to live on?

In many areas, $15/hr is below a living wage for a single adult. It may cover basics in low-cost regions but often falls short for housing, healthcare, and savings in larger cities.
