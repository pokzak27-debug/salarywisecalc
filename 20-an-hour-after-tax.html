Understanding $20 an Hour After Tax

$20/hr full-time puts you in the 12% federal bracket. That's about $3,050 per month or $1,408 biweekly in take-home (before state tax). Use our Salary Budgeting Guide and Cost of Living Guide for planning.

FAQ

How much is $20 an hour after tax monthly?

Roughly $3,050 per month after federal taxes. State tax and withholdings will reduce your actual paycheck.

What's $20/hr after tax biweekly?

About $1,408 per paycheck (26 pay periods). Assumes 40 hrs/week.
