Salary Conversion in Kenya

How Salary Conversion Works in Kenya

In Kenya, full-time work is typically 40–48 hours per week under the Employment Act (8 hours/day, 45 hours/week max). Using 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year, you convert hourly to annual by multiplying: hourly rate × 2,080. For example: KES 1,000 per hour × 2,080 = KES 2,080,000 per year gross. Kenya uses PAYE (Pay As You Earn) progressive income tax: 10% on first KES 24,000/month; 25% on next KES 8,333; 30% above. Personal relief (KES 5,000/month) reduces tax. NSSF (National Social Security Fund), SHIF (Social Health Insurance Fund, 2.75% of gross), and Housing Levy (1.5% of net) also apply. Our calculator uses a simplified illustrative rate; actual take-home depends on your bracket and reliefs. See Gross vs net salary for why gross and take-home differ. Common Pay Frequencies in Kenya

Monthly (12 paydays) is standard. Some employers pay biweekly or weekly. To get monthly gross from annual: divide by 12. See Weekly vs biweekly vs monthly pay for how pay frequency affects budgeting.

What Can Change Take-Home Pay

PAYE bracket (10%, 25%, 30%), personal relief, NSSF tiers, SHIF (2.75%), Housing Levy (1.5% of net), pension contributions, and voluntary deductions affect your paycheck. FAQ

How do I convert hourly pay to yearly salary in Kenya?

Multiply your hourly rate by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks). KES 1,000/hr × 2,080 = KES 2,080,000 per year gross.

What is the standard full-time work week in Kenya?

40–48 hours per week is typical. The Employment Act allows up to 8 hours/day, 45 hours/week.

Does Kenya have income tax on salaries?

Yes. Kenya uses PAYE (10% to 30% progressive), plus NSSF, SHIF (2.75%), and Housing Levy (1.5% of net).

How often do Kenyan employers pay?

Monthly is standard. Some pay biweekly or weekly.

What reduces take-home pay in Kenya?

PAYE income tax, NSSF, SHIF (Social Health Insurance Fund), Housing Levy (1.5%), and pension contributions.
