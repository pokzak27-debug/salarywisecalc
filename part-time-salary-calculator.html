Part-Time Salary Calculator

Part-time work uses fewer hours than full-time (40/week). Multiply hourly rate by hours per week, then by 52 weeks. At $25/hr for 20 hrs/week: $25 × 20 × 52 = $26,000/year gross. Use our calculator below.

Part-Time vs Full-Time

Full-time is typically 40 hrs/week (2,080/year). Part-time varies—20 hrs is half; 30 hrs is 75%. Benefits (health, PTO) often require full-time. Use $20/hr after tax and $55k after tax for full-time equivalents.
