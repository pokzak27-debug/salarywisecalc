Salary to Hourly: Complete Guide

How This Calculation Works

To convert annual salary to hourly wage, divide your yearly pay by the number of hours you work per year. For full-time (40 hours/week, 52 weeks), that's 2,080 hours. So: yearly salary ÷ 2,080 = hourly rate. Example: $60,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $28.85 per hour. Our calculator does this instantly and estimates federal tax on your gross salary.

Example With Real Numbers

A $75,000 yearly salary divided by 2,080 hours equals $36.06 per hour gross. After federal taxes (about 22% at this level), you'd take home roughly $58,500 per year—about $4,875 per month or $2,250 per biweekly paycheck before state tax. Use the calculator above with your salary to see your hourly equivalent and tax estimate.

When This Calculator Is Useful

Use it when comparing a salaried offer to an hourly job, when negotiating (know your hourly worth), or when budgeting—see what each hour of work really earns you. Freelancers can use it to set hourly rates that match a target annual income.

What Can Change the Final Result

Salaried jobs often include benefits (health, 401k) not in the base number. Bonuses and commissions add to gross. The tax estimate is federal only; state tax, deductions, and filing status change take-home. We assume 2,080 hours—actual hours may vary.

FAQ

How do I convert salary to hourly?

Divide your annual salary by 2,080 (40 hrs × 52 weeks). $52,000 ÷ 2,080 = $25 per hour.

What is $70,000 a year per hour?

$70,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $33.65 per hour gross. After federal tax, about $27/hour in take-home.

What is $100,000 a year per hour?

$100,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $48.08 per hour gross. After federal tax, about $36.50/hour take-home.

Is the hourly rate before or after tax?

Our main result is gross (before tax). We also show estimated net annual so you can see take-home.

What if I work more or fewer than 40 hours?

Adjust the divisor. For 50 hrs/week, use 2,600. For 30 hrs/week, use 1,560. The 2,080 standard is for full-time.

Does this include benefits?

No. We use base salary only. Health insurance, 401k match, and PTO have value beyond the hourly number.
