How to Convert Salary to Hourly

To convert annual salary to hourly wage, divide your yearly salary by the number of hours you work per year. For a standard full-time schedule (40 hours per week, 52 weeks), the formula is: annual salary ÷ (40 × 52) = hourly rate.

This helps you compare salaried jobs with hourly positions or understand the real value of your paycheck per hour. Our calculator includes a rough federal tax estimate. Actual take-home pay varies by state and deductions.
