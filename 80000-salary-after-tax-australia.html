AU$80,000 Salary After Tax: Quick Answer

AU$80,000 gross per year. Australian income tax (marginal rates: 0% on first $18,200, 19% to $45,000, 32.5% to $135,000) plus Medicare Levy (2%) leave roughly AU$62,000 take-home. That’s about AU$5,167 per month or AU$2,385 per fortnight. Most Australian employers pay fortnightly. See Gross vs net salary for why gross and net differ. How Tax Affects an AU$80,000 Salary in Australia

Australia has federal income tax only (no state income tax). Marginal rates: 0% to $18,200, 19% to $45,000, 32.5% to $135,000, 37% to $190,000, 45% above. At AU$80,000 you pay 19% on $26,800 and 32.5% on $35,000—income tax ≈ AU$16,500. Medicare Levy is 2% (≈ AU$1,600). Superannuation is employer-funded (11%+); it does not reduce take-home. See Salary Calculator Australia for context.

Example (budgeting): At AU$62,000 net, a common rule is 30% on rent—that’s ~AU$1,550/month. In Brisbane or Adelaide that can rent a decent 1–2 bed; in Sydney or Melbourne it may cover a room or smaller unit. Budget with net, not gross.

What Can Change Your Take-Home Pay

HECS/HELP repayments, salary sacrifice (super, novated lease), tax offsets, Medicare Levy Surcharge (if high income without private health), and deductions all affect your paycheck. Compare gross and after-tax when budgeting—see Compare Salary Offers Guide and Salary Negotiation Guide. Use Salary Comparison Guides for side-by-side numbers.

Example (cost-of-living): AU$62,000 net in Sydney goes less far than in regional areas. In Perth or Hobart, AU$1,500/month rent can get a solid 1BR; in inner Sydney it may cover a share. Research city-specific cost of living when relocating.

Is AU$80,000 a Good Salary in Australia?

AU$80,000 gross (~AU$62,000 net) is above the median full-time income. It’s a solid wage for a single person or small household. In Sydney and Melbourne, cost of living is higher. FAQ

How much is AU$80,000 after tax in Australia?

About AU$62,000 per year after income tax and Medicare Levy. HECS/HELP and salary sacrifice reduce this further.

What is AU$80,000 monthly after tax in Australia?

Roughly AU$5,167 per month after tax. Fortnightly pay is most common in Australia.

Does Medicare Levy affect AU$80,000 take-home in Australia?

Yes. Medicare Levy is 2% of taxable income. At AU$80,000 it adds roughly AU$1,600 to your tax.

How much is AU$80,000 fortnightly after tax?

About AU$2,385 per fortnight after tax. Most Australian employers pay fortnightly.

Is AU$80,000 a good salary in Australia?

AU$80,000 gross (~AU$62,000 net) is above median. Solid for a single person or small household in most cities.

What reduces AU$80,000 take-home in Australia?

Income tax, Medicare Levy, HECS/HELP repayments, salary sacrifice (super, novated lease), and voluntary deductions.

Is super included in AU$80,000?

Super is typically paid by the employer on top of salary (11%+). It does not reduce your take-home pay.
