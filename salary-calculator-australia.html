Salary Conversion in Australia

How Salary Conversion Works in Australia

In Australia, full-time work is often 38 hours per week (under many awards) or 40 hours. Using 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year, you convert hourly to annual by multiplying: hourly rate × 2,080. For example: $30 AUD per hour × 2,080 = $62,400 per year gross. Australia has marginal income tax rates plus Medicare Levy. Superannuation (super) is typically paid by the employer on top of salary (currently 11%+). Our calculator uses a simplified illustrative rate; actual tax depends on your bracket and Medicare status.

Example With Real Numbers

David earns $35 per hour in Sydney. His gross annual salary is $35 × 2,080 = $72,800. After income tax and Medicare Levy (roughly 25–28% at this level), he might keep around $52,000–54,000 per year. That's about $4,300 per month. Super is paid by the employer separately. HECS/HELP repayments would reduce take-home if applicable. The exact amount depends on his tax bracket and any salary sacrifice.

Common Pay Frequencies in Australia

Fortnightly (every 2 weeks, 26 pay periods) is most common. Weekly and monthly pay exist in some roles. Divide annual by 26 for fortnightly, by 52 for weekly, by 12 for monthly.

What Can Change Take-Home Pay

Income tax bracket, Medicare Levy (and surcharge), HECS/HELP repayments, salary sacrifice (super, novated lease), tax offsets, and deductions all affect your paycheck. Super is usually employer-funded and does not reduce take-home.

When This Calculator Is Useful

Use it when comparing an Australian job offer (hourly vs salary), planning a budget, or negotiating pay. Helpful when relocating to Australia. Also useful for contractors converting hourly or day rates to equivalent annual income.

FAQ

How do I convert hourly pay to yearly salary in Australia?

Multiply your hourly rate by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks). $30/hr AUD × 2,080 = $62,400 per year gross.

What is the standard full-time work week in Australia?

38 hours per week is common under many awards. 40 hours is also typical.

Does Australia have income tax on salaries?

Yes. Australia has marginal tax rates and Medicare Levy. Superannuation is additional (employer-funded).

How often do Australian employers pay?

Fortnightly (every 2 weeks) is most common. Weekly and monthly also exist.

What reduces take-home pay in Australia?

Income tax, Medicare Levy, HECS/HELP repayments, salary sacrifice, and voluntary super.
