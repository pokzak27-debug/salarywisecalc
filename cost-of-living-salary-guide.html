Cost of Living vs Salary

Same salary, different lifestyle. A $60k salary goes further in Indianapolis than in San Francisco. This guide explains how cost of living affects your take-home and how to compare locations.

Why Location Matters

Housing, groceries, and taxes vary by city and state. A $70k salary in Austin may feel like $90k in NYC when adjusted for cost of living. Use our $70k after tax and Salary after tax USA for baseline numbers.

Major Cost Drivers

Rent is usually the largest. High-cost metros (NYC, SF, LA, Boston) can double housing costs vs mid-tier cities. State income tax adds another layer—TX and FL have no state income tax; CA and NY do.

Comparing Job Offers

When relocating, compare net pay and local costs. A $10k raise may not cover a move to a pricier city. Use our Salary Comparison Guides and Compare Salary Offers for side-by-side analysis.

FAQ

How does cost of living affect salary?

Same gross salary buys less in high-cost areas. Housing and groceries are the main drivers.

Should I take a pay cut to move to a cheaper city?

Sometimes. Compare net pay and local costs. A lower salary in a cheaper area can mean more disposable income.

Which states have no income tax?

TX, FL, NV, WA, WY, SD, AK, NH (on wages), TN (on wages). More take-home, but other taxes apply.
