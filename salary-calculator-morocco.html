Salary Conversion in Morocco

How Salary Conversion Works in Morocco

In Morocco, full-time work is often 44 hours per week (legal max) or 40 hours in many sectors. Using 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year, you convert hourly to annual by multiplying: hourly rate × 2,080. For example: 50 MAD per hour × 2,080 = 104,000 MAD per year gross. Salaries are commonly quoted monthly—multiply monthly by 12 for annual. Morocco has IR (Impôt sur le Revenu), a progressive income tax, plus CNSS (social security) contributions. Our calculator uses a simplified illustrative rate; actual tax and CNSS depend on your bracket and situation.

Example With Real Numbers

Fatima earns 60 MAD per hour in Casablanca. Her gross annual salary is 60 × 2,080 = 124,800 MAD. That's 10,400 MAD per month gross. After IR and CNSS (roughly 15–25% combined at this level, depending on bracket), she might keep around 95,000–100,000 MAD per year—about 8,000 MAD per month. The exact amount depends on her tax bracket, family situation, and any deductions.

Common Pay Frequencies in Morocco

Monthly pay is standard. Some employers pay twice monthly. Divide annual by 12 for monthly. Job ads often state monthly salary—multiply by 12 for annual comparison.

What Can Change Take-Home Pay

IR (income tax) brackets, CNSS contributions, health insurance, pension, family allowances, and tax deductions (charges déductibles) all affect your paycheck. The IR has several tranches with different rates.

When This Calculator Is Useful

Use it when comparing a Moroccan job offer (hourly vs monthly vs annual), planning a budget, or negotiating pay. Helpful when relocating to Morocco. Also useful for freelancers converting hourly or daily rates to equivalent annual income.

FAQ

How do I convert hourly pay to yearly salary in Morocco?

Multiply your hourly rate by 2,080 (40 hours × 52 weeks). Or: monthly salary × 12 = annual. 50 MAD/hr × 2,080 = 104,000 MAD per year gross.

What is the standard work week in Morocco?

44 hours per week is the legal maximum. 40 hours is common in many sectors.

Does Morocco have income tax?

Yes. IR (Impôt sur le Revenu) has progressive brackets. CNSS (social security) is also deducted.

How often do Moroccan employers pay?

Monthly is standard. Some pay twice monthly.

What reduces take-home pay in Morocco?

IR (income tax), CNSS, health insurance, pension, and other deductions.
